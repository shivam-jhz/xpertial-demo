Process

From Request to Delivery

A transparent 9-step workflow with clear milestones, dual-payment security, and real-time progress tracking. You stay in control at every step.

Start a Project →
01
📋
Submit Your Request
Fill out our 4-step request form — specify data type, volume, format, quality level, and deadline. No commitment required at this stage.
You can describe any custom collection need in plain language. Our team understands AI/ML requirements and will tailor the scope accordingly. Response within 24 hours.
FREE · No card required
02
💬
Receive & Accept Quote
Our team reviews your request and sends a detailed, transparent quote — including per-unit pricing, total cost, timeline breakdown, and collector assignment plan.
All quotes appear in your Client Dashboard. You can ask questions, negotiate scope, or request changes before accepting.
Negotiable · Transparent pricing
03
📜
Sign Data Collection Agreement
A formal contract is generated covering scope, QC standards, IP ownership, confidentiality, and payment terms. Sign digitally from your dashboard.
NDA available for sensitive projects. Upon signing, both parties are legally bound to the agreed deliverables. Data ownership transfers to you upon full payment.
Digital signing · NDA available
04
💳
Pay 50% Deposit
Pay 50% of the project cost upfront via Razorpay — UPI, cards, net banking, or wallets. This activates your project and funds collector assignment.
The deposit is held securely. If XPERTIAL fails to deliver, the deposit is fully refunded. GST invoice generated automatically.
Razorpay secured · GST invoice
05
📡
Data Collection Begins
Our vetted field collectors are assigned to your project and begin collecting per the agreed specifications — locations, conditions, formats, annotation schema.
Track live progress on your dashboard. A dedicated project manager is your point of contact throughout. Collections are uploaded to secure AWS S3.
Live tracking · Dedicated PM
06
🔍
Quality Control Review
Every data point passes through a 3-tier QC process: automated metadata validation, AI model pre-screening, and human expert review.
Items failing QC are automatically rejected and recollected — at no cost to you. Final dataset guaranteed to meet agreed accuracy thresholds.
3-tier QC · 95–99%+ accuracy
07
📦
Dataset Uploaded to Your Panel
The completed, QC-verified dataset is uploaded to your secure AWS S3 storage and appears in your Client Dashboard downloads section.
Files available in your requested format: COCO, YOLO, JSON, CSV, Parquet, WAV+TXT, or custom. You're notified via email.
AWS S3 secure storage · Any format
08
💰
Pay Final 50%
Once satisfied with the dataset preview, pay the remaining 50% via Razorpay to unlock full download access.
You can review the dataset structure and sample files before final payment. Final GST invoice generated. Payment unlocks permanent S3 download links.
Preview first · Then pay
09
✅
Download & Use Your Dataset
Download your complete, verified dataset via pre-signed AWS S3 URLs. Files stored for 90 days — backup recommended.
Full data ownership transfers to you. Need revisions? Raise a ticket from your dashboard. Our QC guarantee covers quality issues reported within 14 days.
Full ownership · 14-day QC guarantee

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take? ▼
Most standard projects (under 50K data points) are completed within 72 hours. Larger projects or complex custom collection tasks may take 1–2 weeks. Rush delivery is available at an additional cost.
What if the data quality doesn't meet my standards? ▼
All datasets come with a 14-day quality guarantee. If data doesn't meet the agreed QC thresholds, we will re-collect and replace the failing items at no additional charge. In severe cases, a partial or full refund is available.
Can I request data from specific locations or demographics? ▼
Yes — geographic targeting is one of our core strengths. You can specify cities, states, rural/urban environments, languages, age groups, genders, and more. Our collector network covers all major Indian states.
Is my project data kept confidential? ▼
All data is stored on encrypted AWS S3 with strict access controls. NDAs are available for sensitive projects. Collectors sign confidentiality agreements and are not aware of the end client's identity.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept all major payment methods via Razorpay: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, and digital wallets. GST invoices are generated automatically.
Do I need a technical background to use XPERTIAL? ▼
Not at all. Our request form guides you through the process in plain language. Your assigned project manager helps translate your AI/ML requirements into a collection plan. You just need to know what data you need.
