Transparency

How Pricing Works

Every project is different. We evaluate six key factors to give you the most accurate, fair quote possible.

🖼️
Data Type
Image, Video, Text, Audio, or Custom. Each type has different collection complexity and equipment requirements.
📊
Volume
The number of data points required. Larger volumes often benefit from lower per-unit costs due to collection efficiency.
📍
Location
City tier, region, and number of distinct locations affect collector deployment cost and availability.
⚙️
Complexity
Simple open-ended tasks vs. strict scenarios with specific conditions, rare environments, or unusual demographics.
🏆
Quality Level
Standard automated QC (~95%), Professional human+auto QC (~98%), or Enterprise gold-standard auditing (~99%+).
⚡
Timeline
Rush delivery within 24h costs more than a relaxed 2-week window. We always meet deadlines, whatever you choose.
