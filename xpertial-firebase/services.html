Image & Video Data

Collect, photograph, record, and annotate visual datasets to your exact specification — lighting, angles, demographics, environments, annotation schema.

Classification Labels

Bounding Boxes

Semantic Segmentation
Pixel-level labeling for scene understanding and autonomous systems.
Instance Segmentation

Keypoint / Pose

Video Labeling

Text & NLP Data

Human-generated and human-annotated text datasets across 20+ Indian and international languages. Perfect for LLM fine-tuning, NER, and classification tasks.

Intent Classification
User queries labeled with intent tags for chatbot and NLU training.
Named Entity Recognition
Person, org, location, date entities marked in natural text.
Sentiment Analysis
Positive, negative, neutral + emotion categories on reviews and posts.
Translation Pairs

Conversational Data
Multi-turn dialogue, Q&A pairs, instruction-following datasets.
Text Generation
Human-written creative content, summaries, rewrites for RLHF data.
